HOW TO PLAY

Learn how to solve the TWISTOPIA puzzle!

1. SELECT A TILE

Click or tap a tile. This selects a 2 x 2 block.

2. ROTATE CLOCKWISE ↻

Press the Clockwise button to rotate the selected block to the right.

3. ROTATE COUNTERCLOCKWISE ↺

Press the Counterclockwise button to rotate the selected block to the left.

4. COMPLETE THE PUZZLE

Keep rotating the tiles until the original picture or number arrangement is complete!
